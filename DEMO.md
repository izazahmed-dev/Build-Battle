# InsightFlow — three-minute demonstration

**Opening:** “What should a team fix next, and can we trace the recommendation back to what customers actually said?”

1. **0:00–0:30 — Reveal the data.** Open Overview. Explain that the supplied file has 7,000 rows but 320 distinct texts. Switch counting modes. These are review texts, not verified independent customers. Point to sentiment and analysis coverage.
2. **0:30–1:00 — Make a decision inspectable.** Open seller trust and payment safety. Show the policy formula, original quotes, and matching-text count. Track the issue with Trust & Safety as owner. The action proposes verification, not a conclusion that fraud occurred.
3. **1:00–1:40 — Show real AI work.** Open Adaption studio. Show a completed batch, its provider dataset/run ID, quote, accepted/rejected result counts, and downloadable output. Explain that Adaptive Data improves structured baseline completions using original review context. Never claim a pending batch has completed.
4. **1:40–2:10 — Keep a person in control.** Open a mixed complaint or negated-fraud review in the inbox. Show its supporting quote and provider lineage. Explain exact-quote validation and the human-review correction flow. A valid quote alone does not prove a label is accurate.
5. **2:10–2:40 — Carry the insight into action.** Open Issue tracker and Reports. Show durable saving, ownership, and export. Keep tasks Planned until a team actually works on them.
6. **2:40–3:00 — Close with honest architecture.** Show Architecture. Explain private server storage and conflict protection. No dates were supplied, so no invented historical trend is shown; a genuine dated import enables the chart. End on the next decision and its evidence.
